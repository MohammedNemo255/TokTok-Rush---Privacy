TokTok Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

TokTok Rush ("the game", "the app") is published by NemoDroid ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, and what choices you have. Questions or requests: nemonegm@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • If you want to cash out coins, you sign in with Google and give us payout details so we can pay you.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and sells optional items through Google Play.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Game progress and settings: coins, best distance, upgrades, skins, missions, sound, music and language Always To save your game. It stays on your phone. Only the totals listed in the cash-out row below are ever sent to us.
Google account details: name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID Only if you sign in with Google So your cash-out requests belong to your account and you can see them again on a new phone. We never receive your Google password. Your picture is loaded from Google's servers to show it in the game.
Cash-out requests: amount, coins used, wallet phone number or InstaPay address, the name on that account, date and status of the request, plus your lifetime coins earned, coins spent and best distance Only when you send a request To pay you, to check the request is genuine and to prevent fraud and abuse.
Advertising identifier, device information and IP address While ads are shown Google AdMob uses them to show, limit and measure ads.
Purchases (which items you bought) When you buy something Handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
Technical data: app installation identifiers, IP address and basic diagnostics Automatically Processed by Google services we use (Firebase and Google Play services) to keep the game working and secure.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To let you sign in, send cash-out requests, and see their status (waiting, paid or rejected).
  • To pay you by mobile wallet or InstaPay, and to return coins if a request is rejected.
  • To show ads and to process the purchases you make.
  • To detect and prevent cheating, fraud and abuse.

4. Who receives your information

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, AdMob and Google Play) processes information on our behalf and for its own purposes as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Our payment team sees your cash-out requests in order to pay them.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users from fraud.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Game progress stays on your phone until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
  • Cash-out requests and related account details are kept as long as needed to pay you and to meet our accounting and fraud-prevention needs, then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Sign out at any time in Settings.
  • Access, correct or delete your information by emailing nemonegm@gmail.com from the Google account you used. We will reply within a reasonable time.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law, or the GDPR in the European Union) you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to use these rights.

7. Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 18, use cash-out only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Access rules make sure each player can see only their own cash-out requests. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

10. Contact

NemoDroid — nemonegm@gmail.com
